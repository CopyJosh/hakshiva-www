Child-Parent Clinic (Ages 6-12)

Therapy for Children Ages 6–12

Hakshiva Child Parent Clinic

The years between ages 6 and 12 are a significant stage in a child's development. As children navigate increasing academic expectations, peer relationships, emotional growth, and growing independence, they may experience challenges that affect their wellbeing at home, in school, and within their social world.

At Hakshiva Child Parent Clinic, we view these challenges within the broader context of the child's family, relationships, and environment. We believe that meaningful and lasting change occurs when parents are empowered to play an active role in their child's therapeutic process.

Schedule Intake

Our Approach

Our clinic offers child therapy through a holistic, family-centered model. Rather than focusing solely on the symptoms a child presents, we seek to understand the underlying factors contributing to those difficulties and how they relate to the child's emotional, relational, and developmental needs.

A core principle of our work is that parents are the most important resource in a child's life. By supporting and empowering parents, we help create sustainable change that extends beyond the therapy room and into everyday family life.

Our work places a strong emphasis on attachment, family relationships, and strengthening the parent-child connection as a foundation for healthy development.

How the Program Works

Every family begins with a comprehensive intake process led by a managing social worker. During this process, we gather information about family dynamics, attachment patterns, challenges, strengths, and goals. Together with the parents, we develop an individualized treatment plan designed to address the unique needs of the child and family.

When appropriate, we may recommend additional services or develop a coordinated multi-service plan that integrates different therapeutic approaches and professionals. Our goal is always to ensure that each family receives the support that will be most beneficial and effective for their specific circumstances.

Each family is assigned a dedicated Case Manager who coordinates the therapeutic process, facilitates communication among professionals, and helps ensure that everyone is working toward shared goals. Through ongoing staff consultations, supervision, and regular reviews, we maintain a collaborative approach that allows the treatment plan to evolve as the family's needs change.

Therapeutic Services

Our tailored, multi-disciplinary plans combine specific modalities to best support your family:

Parenting Therapy

An attachment-focused approach that empowers parents to understand their child's needs, navigate challenges, and lead family change with confidence.

Parent-Child (Dyadic) Therapy

Joint sessions for parents and children designed to strengthen relationships, improve communication, and build secure attachments.

Creative & Expressive Therapies

Safe environments for self-discovery and emotional expression using a child's natural language, including:

  • • Art Therapy
  • • Music Therapy
  • • Psychodrama
  • • Play Therapy

Community Education & Support

Outreach programs, workshops, and seminars for parents and educators to raise mental health awareness and reduce stigma.

Our Mentoring Programs

One-on-One Mentoring

Focused on emotional well-being, this program pairs children with mentors for twice-weekly sessions built around the child’s interests. Whether through music, art, playing games or simply hanging out, children gain a safe and supportive space to grow.

Our school-based mentors provide academic and emotional support directly within the school setting, helping students build confidence and succeed in the classroom.

Collaborative Approach: We work closely with teachers and school counselors to create individualized support plans.

Our Approach: Social Thinking

Our framework goes beyond surface-level actions to teach the “why” behind social behaviour. By focusing on Social Thinking, we help children understand the connection between thoughts, feelings, and actions. Our coaches translate abstract social concepts into concrete, actionable skills, helping kids navigate group settings, build lasting friendships, and manage complex peer dynamics.

Each program begins with a comprehensive assessment—including parent/teacher consultations and social observations—to create a tailor-made treatment plan. Supervised by clinical experts and backed by overwhelmingly positive feedback, our goal is to facilitate lasting social success.

Through personalized one-on-one coaching for boys and girls ages 5+, we empower them to:

Navigate group settings with confidence.

Olim Services

Aliyah Integration & Support

Making Aliya to Israel can bring significant upheaval and changes for children. The language, curriculum, cultural mentality, and study habits are vastly different from their country of origin.

Recognizing the very real challenges faced during the initial years of Aliyah, Hakshiva has served as an anchor and a safe haven for Olim students in schools since 2018.

Schedule Intake

Individualized Accompaniment

Around 50 students enjoy two hours of weekly, one-on-one learning within school, led by a remedial teacher who serves as both an emotional and academic anchor.

Emotional Support System

Dedicated gatherings for the students and their families, providing a containing, embracing, and tailored response to the challenges of Aliyah.

Our Goal

A smooth and optimal integration of the students into the education system, while creating a stable and secure foundation for the entire family.
